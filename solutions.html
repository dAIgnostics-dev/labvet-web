Solutions

AI solutions for veterinary diagnostics

From the moment an animal is booked in to the signed report and the settled invoice. Built for practices, referral clinics and veterinary pathology laboratories.

Solution 01

Pathology workflow, end to end

  • dAI Edge
  • dAI DataHub
  • dAI Clarity

Accession specimens the moment they arrive and follow every block, slide and stain through grossing, processing, cutting and staining, with cytology, histopathology, haematology, biochemistry, necropsy and microbiology in one case record.

  • Accessioning at arrival, with the animal, owner, referring vet and submission form linked to the case
  • Block, slide and stain tracking through grossing, processing, cutting and staining, with the location of every case visible without walking to the bench
  • One case record across disciplines: cytology, histopathology, haematology, biochemistry, necropsy and microbiology
  • Sign-out queues per pathologist, with turnaround visible for every open case

Solution 02

Digital pathology and image handling

  • dAI DataHub
  • dAI Inference
  • dAI Clarity

Whole-slide images stored with the case they belong to, compared side by side across timepoints, annotated and measured on the slide, and screened by AI as a second pair of eyes.

  • Whole-slide images stored alongside the case, never as loose files
  • Side-by-side comparison of slides across timepoints, with annotation and measurement on the slide
  • AI-assisted screening for mitotic counts, cellularity and regions worth a closer look, always flagged for the pathologist and never entered as the diagnosis
  • Every AI suggestion explained and traceable to the model version that produced it

Solution 03

Diagnostic pipeline automation

  • dAI Edge
  • dAI DataHub
  • dAI Inference

In-house analysers, slide scanners and external reference laboratories connected in one flow: results and metadata imported automatically, checked on arrival, and flagged against species-, breed- and age-appropriate reference ranges.

  • Connectors for in-house analysers, slide scanners and external reference laboratories
  • Automatic import of results and metadata, with technical checks and quality control on arrival
  • Reference ranges by species, breed and age: a Greyhound's haematology, a cat's thyroid panel and a horse's biochemistry are not read against the same numbers
  • Out-of-range values flagged before anyone has to look for them

Solution 04

Animal, owner and document data

  • dAI Edge
  • dAI DataHub

Every result linked to the animal, the owner, the referring vet, the analyser, the sample and the protocol version, with the paperwork checked before it holds up a case.

  • One record per animal: species, breed, age, sex, neuter status, weight, microchip and full clinical history, from a household pet to a production herd
  • Versioned, searchable and audit-ready records with role-based access
  • Automated checking of intake and submission forms, consent for anaesthesia, biopsy and necropsy, referral letters, vaccination and microchip records
  • Export and health certificates and insurance paperwork validated for completeness and consistency

Solution 05

Scheduling, billing and claims

  • dAI Inference
  • dAI Clarity

Consultations, surgery slots, sampling appointments, staff rotas, pathologist workload, analyser and scanner availability, hospitalisation and boarding capacity and recall reminders planned in one place, and every case tracked from estimate to invoice.

  • One calendar for consultations, surgery, sampling, staff rotas and sign-out queues
  • Analyser, scanner, hospitalisation and boarding capacity planned together, with recall reminders
  • Consultations, procedures, medications, panels and pathology cases tracked from estimate to invoice
  • Insurance claims prepared from the clinical record, with no manual reconciliation at the end of the month

Solution 06

Automated reports and owner summaries

  • dAI Inference
  • dAI Clarity

Gross and microscopic descriptions, diagnoses, prognostic comments, discharge instructions and owner-facing summaries drafted from the data and the pathologist's findings, ready for review and sign-out.

  • Drafts of gross descriptions, microscopic descriptions, diagnoses and prognostic comments from the underlying data
  • The same case phrased for the referring clinician and, separately, for the animal's owner
  • Statistical and AI processing with trend analysis across the animal's history and across the cases in your archive
  • Results presented with confidence, uncertainty and full traceability, never an unexplained score
